# Information sources

Canonical sources the skill consults. Organized by purpose; use whichever fits the question. Always cite in the report's Sources section.

## Pricing / aggregators

### Cross-region price-history tools
- [camelcamelcamel](https://camelcamelcamel.com) — Amazon US/UK/DE/FR/IT/ES/CA/JP/AU/MX price history, free, full
- [Keepa](https://keepa.com) — 11 Amazon marketplaces, deeper data, paid
- [PCPartPicker](https://pcpartpicker.com) — PC components with native price history (US/UK/DE/CA/AU/FR/IT/ES + others)

### Regional aggregators
- **US**: Google Shopping, [Newegg](https://www.newegg.com), [B&H Photo Video](https://www.bhphotovideo.com), [Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net) (deal-flagging community)
- **UK**: [PriceSpy UK](https://pricespy.co.uk), [PriceRunner](https://www.pricerunner.com), Google Shopping; primary marketplaces: Amazon UK, Currys, John Lewis, Argos
- **EU**: [Idealo](https://www.idealo.de) (DE/AT/IT/FR/ES), [Geizhals](https://geizhals.de) (DE/AT — PC + electronics deep), [PriceSpy Nordics](https://prisjakt.nu); primary marketplaces: Amazon DE/FR/IT/ES, MediaMarkt, Saturn, Otto, Fnac
- **Canada**: [PCPartPicker.ca](https://pcpartpicker.com/ca), [Newegg.ca](https://newegg.ca), [RedFlagDeals](https://www.redflagdeals.com); Amazon.ca, Best Buy, Canada Computers
- **Australia / NZ**: [StaticICE](https://www.staticice.com.au), [PCPartPicker AU](https://pcpartpicker.com/au), [Shopbot](https://www.shopbot.com.au); Amazon.au, JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks
- **Japan**: [Kakaku.com](https://kakaku.com) — Japan's primary price comparison; Amazon.co.jp, Rakuten, Yodobashi
- **India**: [Mysmartprice](https://www.mysmartprice.com), [PriceDekho](https://www.pricedekho.com), [Compare India](https://www.compareraja.in); Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma
- **Brazil**: [Buscapé](https://www.buscape.com.br), [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com.br); Amazon.br, Mercado Livre, Magazine Luiza
- **MENA (UAE, KSA, EG, etc.)**: [Yaoota](https://yaoota.com); Amazon.ae/.sa, Noon, Carrefour
- **China**: Tmall, JD.com (region-specific catalogs and pricing)
- **Turkey**: [Akakce](https://www.akakce.com), [Cimri](https://www.cimri.com); Amazon.com.tr, Hepsiburada, Trendyol, N11
- **Other regions**: search `"<category> price comparison <country>"` to identify the local aggregator(s); most countries with mid+ e-commerce maturity have one.

**Universal caveat**: aggregators surface listing price, not transacted price; many do not subtract coupons/loyalty. Manual spot-check at the seller page is mandatory.

## Compatibility & standards

- **Manufacturer compatibility charts** — Shimano, SRAM, ASUS QVL, etc. First source.
- [ISO Online Browsing Platform](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/) — ISO standards (paywalled for full text, free abstracts)
- [IEC](https://www.iec.ch), [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org), [EN standards via DIN](https://www.din.de) — engineering standards
- Forum corroboration — Reddit + Stack Exchange + manufacturer-specific forums; require ≥2 independent sources

## Compliance & safety

- [EPREL](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/eprel_en) — EU energy-label database (mandatory)
- [CPSC Recalls](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls), [SaferProducts.gov](https://www.saferproducts.gov/) — US recall registries
- [EU Safety Gate (RAPEX)](https://ec.europa.eu/safety-gate-alerts/) — EU recall alerts
- [NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov) — US vehicle recalls
- Manufacturer recall pages — direct from brand
- Regional consumer-protection authorities — each country has one (e.g., FTC in US, CMA in UK, BEUC + national regulators in EU, ACCC in Australia, CCPC in Ireland, etc.). Search `"<country> consumer protection authority"`.

## Customs / cross-border

Always use the destination country's official customs authority for binding duty rates and rules. Examples:
- **US**: [USITC Tariff Database](https://hts.usitc.gov), [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov) — duty rates + de minimis rules
- **EU (any member)**: [TARIC](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/taric/) — EU-wide tariff schedule; VAT/excise applied at member-state level
- **UK**: [gov.uk Trade Tariff](https://www.gov.uk/trade-tariff) — UK tariff post-Brexit
- **Canada**: [CBSA Tariff Finder](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/menu-eng.html)
- **Australia**: [ABF tariff working pages](https://www.abf.gov.au/importing-exporting-and-manufacturing/tariff-classification)
- **Japan**: [Japan Customs](https://www.customs.go.jp)
- **India**: [CBIC Tariff](https://www.cbic-gst.gov.in)
- **Turkey**: [Ticaret.gov.tr](https://ticaret.gov.tr)
- **Other countries**: search `"<country> customs duty calculator"` and prefer the government source over third-party calculators
- **FX cross-check**: use the user-region's central bank effective rate (e.g., Federal Reserve H.10 for USD, ECB for EUR, BoE for GBP, RBA for AUD, etc.) to detect FX margin manipulation in cross-border listings
- **De minimis trend**: many countries have lowered or removed low-value exemptions in 2024–2026; assume full duty + VAT applies unless verified for the user's region as of the run date.

## Manufacturer datasheets (primary source)

For any candidate, always pull the manufacturer's official product page and datasheet. Manufacturer claims are not gospel (especially on marketing-driven specs), but they are the canonical "what this product is supposed to be" reference and the basis for warranty disputes.

## Citation discipline reminder

- Standards/compat claims: ≥2 independent sources.
- Opinions/reviews: ≥1 source.
- Price data: ≥1 aggregator + ≥1 primary seller.
- Every URL goes in the report's Sources section, numbered.
