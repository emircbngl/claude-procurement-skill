# Domain: Furniture (chairs, desks, sofas, beds, mattresses, office, ergonomic)

Highest-purchase-regret category in many consumer surveys — visual matters fail at home, ergonomic claims aren't testable in showroom, materials drive durability.

## Research dimensions
- **functional_specs**: dimensions (W × D × H + seat depth + seat height), weight, weight capacity, materials (frame: solid wood / engineered / metal; upholstery: leather grade / fabric Martindale rub count; foam: density kg/m³ + ILD firmness), assembly required, warranty length per component
- **quality_signals**: brand reliability (BIFMA-tested for office chairs, ANSI-tested for desks), independent reviews (Wirecutter, RTINGS for office chairs, Sleep Foundation for mattresses), owner long-term reviews at 2+yr
- **economic**: purchase + delivery + assembly + reupholstering (sofas 8–12yr), replacement (cheap chair every 2yr vs Herman Miller 12yr)
- **usage_fit**: home office daily 8hr (ergonomic critical) / occasional desk / lounging / dining / hosting / bedroom / kids / outdoor

## Required user inputs (overrides universal)
- **Room dimensions** + door / hallway / elevator clearance (delivery fail = restock fee)
- **User anthropometry** for ergonomics (height, weight, sit-bone width for saddle seats)
- **Use intensity** (8hr/day vs 2hr/day determines required tier)
- **Aesthetic constraint** (color, material, style — strong veto power)
- **Pet / kid factor** (pet-claws need tough upholstery; spills need washable / leather)
- **Rental status** (can't drill walls = no wall-mounted; movability matters)

## Standards & compatibility axes

| Axis | Values | Compat rule |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Frame material** | Solid hardwood (oak/walnut), engineered (plywood best, MDF medium, particleboard worst), metal (steel powder-coat) | Particleboard fails at 2–5yr load cycles; plywood lasts decades; solid wood best but expensive |
| **Upholstery durability (fabric)** | Martindale rub count — 10k = household low, 20k = household high, 40k+ = commercial | Heavy daily use needs 30k+; pet households 40k+ |
| **Leather grade** | Full-grain (best, ages well) → top-grain → split / bonded (poor) | "Bonded leather" is glued scraps; fails in 2–5yr |
| **Foam density** | kg/m³ — 25 = poor, 35 = household, 45+ = commercial / firm | Higher density holds shape longer; firmness (ILD) is separate axis from density |
| **Office chair certification** | BIFMA X5.1 (US, durability), DIN EN 1335 (EU ergonomic), GS mark | BIFMA-rated = commercial-grade durability claim |
| **Mattress firmness scale** | 1–10 (1 ultra-soft, 10 firm); brand-specific | Sleep position determines optimal: side 4–6, back 5–7, stomach 6–8 |
| **Mattress construction** | innerspring / pocket coil / latex / memory foam / hybrid | Hybrid (coil + foam top) most universally rated; pure foam sleeps hot |
| **Mattress cover** | washable / non-washable; cooling fabric (TENCEL, Cooling cover) | Allergen-prone or kid bed: washable cover mandatory |
| **Bed frame standard sizes (TR)** | Single 90×190, Double 120×200, Queen 150×200, King 180×200, Super King 200×200 | Mattress size must match frame; TR vs EU vs US sizes differ |
| **Sofa modularity** | Sectional / modular / fixed | Modular = move/reconfig friendly; fixed = lower price + tighter build |
| **Standing desk** | Single motor / dual motor; max height; lifting capacity kg; programmable presets | Dual motor smoother + higher capacity; single fine for solo use |
| **Monitor arm VESA** | 75×75, 100×100, larger for big monitors | Arm must match monitor's VESA pattern |

## Common pitfalls

- **Sofa doesn't fit through door / elevator** — measure door, elevator, hallway turns BEFORE ordering. Delivery teams charge restock + return shipping.
- **Third-party-marketplace "Herman Miller" / "Steelcase" / "Eames" replicas** — pervasive on AliExpress and on most regional marketplace third-party sellers; knockoffs with terrible mechanism reliability and not the real product. Buy iconic-design furniture only through authorized dealers (lookup on brand's official site).
- **Particleboard MDF "wood" furniture** — visually OK but fails on disassembly/reassembly (screws strip). Plywood far better at slight price premium.
- **Mattress without trial period** — sleep-feel ≠ store-feel. Look for 30–100 day return policy.
- **Office chair without arm height / depth adjustability** — most cheap "ergonomic" chairs are not adjustable enough. Aeron / Embody / Diffrient / Soul mostly adjustable; cheap copies often not.
- **Standing desk capacity exceeded** — monitor + arms + accessories can easily hit 30kg; entry-tier desks rated 80kg may struggle.
- **Lumbar support fixed at wrong height** — for users < 165cm or > 185cm, chair lumbar often doesn't hit lower back; check user-height range in spec.
- **Reupholstery cost ignored** — quality sofa at 8–12yr needs reupholstery (often as expensive as new mid-tier sofa); factor into TCO.

## Mattress-specific

- 90+ day trial period is now industry standard for direct-to-consumer mattress brands across most major markets.
- Sleep Foundation, Wirecutter, RTINGS have aggregated independent testing.
- Trial = ~30 nights before body adapts; don't return at week 1.
- Foundation matters — old slats can ruin a good mattress.

## Regional notes

- **Local-brand strength**: every region has dominant local furniture brands (often mid-tier with wide distribution); identify the user-region's equivalent. Quality varies widely within a single brand's lineup — review per-product, not per-brand.
- **IKEA global**: design + flat-pack pricing tier in most major markets; quality acceptable at the price point; not premium.
- **Premium imports** (Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Vitra, Carl Hansen, B&B Italia, etc.): usually via authorized regional dealer or design showroom; verify warranty + service in-country.
- **Custom / bespoke**: regional craftsmanship varies — get references and inspect prior work before commissioning.
- **Mattress DTC**: most regions now have direct-to-consumer mattress brands with trial periods (Casper, Purple, Tuft & Needle in US; Eve, Otty, Simba in UK/EU; regional equivalents elsewhere); compare TCO vs traditional showroom purchase.

## B2B variant (office / hospitality / contract)
- **Contract-grade** materials required (Martindale 40k+, BIFMA-tested chairs)
- Bulk pricing tiered (10+ desks, 50+ chairs)
- Centralized installation / service contracts
- Warranty extended to commercial-use coverage (some consumer warranty void for office use)
- Compliance: fire-retardant ratings for hotels, schools (EN 1021 cigarette + match for upholstery)
- Sustainability certifications often required: GreenGuard, Cradle to Cradle, LEED contribution
